# Front pages

The pages published at the root of the site: QuantumPulse Analytics presented page by page, with
the numbers taken from the firm's own records as of 12 Oct 2026. They were assembled
by the operator, not by the agents, and no agent edits this folder.

The agents' own site, rebuilt on every shift, is published under `console/` from
`site/`. The publishing workflow puts the two together.

The pages run on plain HTML: each one carries its template and its logic, and
`assets/dc.js` (with Preact) draws them in the browser. Fonts are self-hosted under
the SIL Open Font License.
